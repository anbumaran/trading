Trend Template (Stage 2 uptrend)
•	Price is above both the 150-day and 200-day moving averages.
•	The 150-day MA is above the 200-day MA.
•	The 200-day MA has been trending up for at least 1 month (ideally 4–5 months).
•	The 50-day MA is above both the 150-day and 200-day MAs.
•	Price is above the 50-day MA.
•	Price is at least 30% above its 52-week low (many traders prefer 40–100%+).
•	Price is within 25% of its 52-week high (closer is better).
•	Relative Strength rating is 70 or higher (ideally 80–90+), meaning the stock is outperforming most of the market.

Fundamentals (the “SEPA” part)
•	Current quarterly EPS growth of at least 25%, ideally accelerating quarter over quarter.
•	Sales growth of at least 20–25%.
•	Expanding profit margins.
•	Leadership in a strong industry group, with a new product or catalyst as a bonus.

VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern) base
•	The stock has a prior uptrend, then a base of at least 3 weeks (typically 3–65 weeks).
•	It shows 2–6 successive pullbacks (contractions), each shallower than the one before, e.g. 25% → 12% → 6% → 3%.
•	Volume dries up as the base tightens, with the quietest days at the right edge.
•	The final contraction is tight (roughly under 10%), with price closing near the highs of the range.

Entry trigger
•	Buy on a breakout above the pivot point (the high of the final contraction).
•	The breakout day’s volume is at least 40–50% above the 50-day average volume.
•	Avoid stocks extended more than 5% above the pivot.

8. Risk rules
•	Initial stop of 7–8% below entry, tighter if the pattern is tight.
•	Aim for a risk-to-reward ratio of at least 1:3.
•	Trade only liquid stocks, with enough average volume and a minimum price.
